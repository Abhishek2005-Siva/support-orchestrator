# Lost or stolen card

- Tell us as soon as you notice. We can **block the card immediately**; after that no new payments or withdrawals are authorised.
- If you report a card lost or stolen **before it is used**, your liability is **$0**. If it was used without your permission we dispute those payments (see report fraud).
- Blocking is permanent for a card reported lost or stolen. Ask for a **replacement card**: it arrives in **5-7 business days** and is activated in the app.
- If you find the card, you still need the replacement; a blocked card cannot be reactivated.
