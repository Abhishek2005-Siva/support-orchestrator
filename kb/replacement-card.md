# Replacement and spare cards

- A replacement is issued for a card that is **blocked, lost or expired**. It arrives in **5-7 business days**.
- The replacement keeps your account and limits but has a new card number. Update the number with merchants that bill you automatically.
- A card that is still active cannot be replaced; block it first if you want a new one because of damage or suspected compromise.
