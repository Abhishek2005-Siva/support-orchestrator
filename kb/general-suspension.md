# Account suspension and reactivation

Accounts are suspended for unpaid invoices (after 14 days of failed payment), terms of service violations, or at the owner's request. A suspended workspace is read-only and API calls return 403. For payment suspensions pay the outstanding invoice or update the card and click Retry payment; the account reactivates within 15 minutes. Other suspensions require a review by the trust team (up to 2 business days).
