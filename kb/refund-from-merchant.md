# A merchant refund has not appeared

- Merchant refunds are sent by the merchant and take **5-10 business days** to appear on a card.
- The refund shows as a credit with the merchant's name. Check pending transactions too.
- If the merchant confirms they refunded you and 10 business days have passed, contact us with their refund reference.
