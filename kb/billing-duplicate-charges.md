# Duplicate or double charges

A duplicate charge means two successful payments for the same plan period (same amount, issued within 48 hours of each other). Duplicates are always eligible for a full refund of the later charge if reported within 90 days, regardless of the normal refund window.

Duplicate refunds are verified against billing records and processed in 5-10 business days to the original card. You do not need to dispute the charge with your bank. Refunds above $100 require approval by a human billing specialist before they are issued.
