# Provisional credit while we investigate

- Provisional credit is money placed back in your account while a dispute is investigated.
- It is issued **immediately** for disputes of **$500 or less** when your identity is verified, the account is older than **30 days** and no internal review applies.
- Above $500, or if those conditions are not met, a **human specialist approves** the provisional credit first (usually within 1 business day). Until it is approved no credit is posted.
- If the investigation concludes the charge was valid, the provisional credit is reversed after notice.
