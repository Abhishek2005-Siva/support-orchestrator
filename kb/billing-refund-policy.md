# Refund policy

- **Monthly plans:** refundable in full within **30 days** of the payment date.
- **Annual plans:** refundable in full within **14 days** of the payment date.
- **Duplicate charges:** refundable within **90 days** (see duplicate charges).
- Failed or unpaid invoices have nothing to refund. An invoice that was already refunded cannot be refunded again.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method only; we cannot refund to a different card or as cash.
- Refunds of **$100 or less** are approved automatically after eligibility is verified. Refunds **over $100** require manual approval by a human billing specialist (usually within 1 business day).
- Agents and support staff can never guarantee a refund before eligibility has been verified against billing records.
