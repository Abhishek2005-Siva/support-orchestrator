# When and how we escalate to a human

Conversations are escalated to a human specialist when: the customer is very upset or asks for a person, the issue involves legal threats, data breaches or account security, a refund above $100 needs approval, the customer has contacted us 3 or more times about the same issue, or the assistant is not confident enough in an answer. A human reviews escalated cases within 1 business day for Standard, 4 hours for Premium and 1 hour for VIP customers during support hours.
