# Promo codes and account credits

Promo codes are entered at checkout or in Dashboard > Billing > Promo code and apply to the next invoice only. A code can be used once per account and cannot be combined with another code. Account credits (for example SLA credits or goodwill credits) are applied automatically against the next invoice and do not expire for 12 months; credits are not payable as cash.
