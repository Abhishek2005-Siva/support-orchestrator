# Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Business and Enterprise customers can also pay by bank transfer (ACH or SEPA) on annual contracts.

To update your card: Dashboard > Billing > Payment method > Update card. The new card is used for the next charge; to retry an unpaid invoice immediately choose "Retry payment" after updating.
Cards that expire are flagged 30 days beforehand by email. Support staff never ask for a full card number, CVV or password in chat or email; only the last four digits are ever shown.
