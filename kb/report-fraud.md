# Report a payment you do not recognise (fraud)

- First check the merchant name, the date and the city. Many merchants bill under a different trading name, and subscriptions repeat monthly.
- If you did not make or authorise the payment, tell us. We verify it against your account, and a payment is treated as likely fraud when it shows a **fraud signal**: the card was used in a **foreign country**, **3 or more transactions on one card within an hour**, an online payment at a **high-risk merchant category**, or our fraud monitoring had already flagged it.
- When there are fraud signals we **block the card**, **dispute the payment as unauthorised** and offer a **replacement card**.
- Without fraud signals we can still dispute the payment; the card stays active.
- Report within **60 days** of the payment.
