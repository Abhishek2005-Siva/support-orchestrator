# Uptime SLA and service credits

Business and Enterprise plans have a **99.9% monthly uptime SLA** for the API. If monthly uptime falls below 99.9% but at least 99.0%, you receive a 10% credit of that month's fee; below 99.0%, 25%. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the month and are applied to the next invoice. Scheduled maintenance announced 72 hours in advance is excluded.
