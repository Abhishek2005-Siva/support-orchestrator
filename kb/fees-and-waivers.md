# Fees and fee waivers

- Common fees: overdraft fee ($35), extended overdraft fee ($50), returned item fee ($35), out-of-network ATM fee, monthly maintenance fee, wire fee ($25).
- **Courtesy waiver:** one fee can be waived per customer in any **12 months**, up to **$35**, if the account is in good standing and the fee is less than **60 days old**.
- Fees above $35 are reviewed by a human specialist.
- A fee that has already been reversed cannot be reversed again.
- Fees are shown in your transactions as 'Fee'; a waiver appears as a credit.
