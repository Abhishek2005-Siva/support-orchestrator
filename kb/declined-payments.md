# Why a card payment was declined

- **Insufficient funds:** the balance plus any overdraft is lower than the payment.
- **Daily limit:** the payment would take you over the card's daily limit ($2,500 spend, $800 ATM).
- **International payments disabled:** the card is not enabled for payments abroad; you can turn this on in the app.
- **Suspected fraud:** fraud monitoring paused the payment; confirm the payment in the app or contact us.
- **Card blocked or expired:** the card cannot be used; request a replacement.
- **Wrong PIN:** three wrong PIN entries block PIN use until reset.
