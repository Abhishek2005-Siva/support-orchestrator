# Taxes, VAT and tax IDs

VAT/GST is added for customers in the EU, UK, India, Australia and other regions where it is required. To add a VAT/GST number go to Dashboard > Billing > Tax information; it applies to future invoices. We can reissue a past invoice with a tax ID within 12 months. US sales tax is calculated by state where applicable.
