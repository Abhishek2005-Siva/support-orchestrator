# Pending payments and authorisation holds

- A **pending** payment is an authorisation hold: the merchant has reserved the money but not yet collected it.
- Holds usually disappear or turn into a posted payment within **5 business days**. Fuel stations, hotels and restaurants often place holds larger than the final amount.
- A pending hold and a posted payment for the same purchase are **not** two charges. You do not need to dispute a pending hold.
- If a hold is still pending after 5 business days, a specialist can ask the merchant to release it.
