# Charged twice at a merchant (duplicate charge)

- A **duplicate charge** is two card purchases on the same card, at the same merchant, for the same amount, within **24 hours**.
- Before disputing, check whether one of them is only a **pending authorisation hold**. A hold plus a posted charge for the same purchase is one charge shown twice; the hold drops off by itself within **5 business days**.
- If **both charges have posted**, the later one can be disputed as a duplicate. The dispute must be raised within **60 days** of the transaction.
- Customers whose identity is verified, whose account is older than 30 days and whose amount is **$500 or less** receive **provisional credit immediately**. Larger amounts need approval by a human specialist (usually within 1 business day).
- Two purchases of the same amount at the same merchant on different days are normal repeat purchases, not duplicates.
- A duplicate that was already disputed or credited cannot be disputed again.
