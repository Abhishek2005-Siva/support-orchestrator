# Billing disputes and chargebacks

Please contact support before filing a chargeback with your bank; most billing issues are fixed within 1 business day. Filing a chargeback freezes the account until the dispute is resolved, and a lost chargeback can lead to suspension. If you believe a charge is fraudulent, tell us immediately so we can lock the account and investigate; unauthorised charges are refunded in full once confirmed.
