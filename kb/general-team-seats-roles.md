# Team members, seats and roles

Roles: **Owner** (everything incl. billing), **Admin** (manage members and settings, no ownership transfer), **Member** (create dashboards and use the API), **Viewer** (read-only). Invite members in Dashboard > Settings > Team. Seat limits per plan: Free 1, Starter 3, Pro 10, Business 50, Enterprise unlimited. Removing a member frees the seat immediately.
