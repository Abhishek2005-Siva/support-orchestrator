# How to dispute a transaction

- You can dispute a card transaction within **60 days** of the transaction date (Reg E).
- Reasons we handle: **duplicate charge**, **unauthorised payment** (not made by you), goods not received, wrong amount.
- We first verify the transaction against your account records, then file the dispute. You will receive a dispute id (DSP-...).
- We investigate and confirm the outcome within **10 business days**.
- You do not need to contact the merchant first for a duplicate or unauthorised payment. For goods not received, try the merchant first if you can.
- A transaction that already has a dispute or a refund cannot be disputed again.
