# ATM withdrawals

- Daily ATM limit: **$800**.
- Orbit Bank ATMs are free. Out-of-network ATMs may charge an **out-of-network fee** plus the ATM owner's fee.
- If the machine kept your card or gave the wrong amount, report it with the ATM location and time; we investigate with the ATM operator.
