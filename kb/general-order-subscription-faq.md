# Subscription and order basics

Orbit is a subscription product, so there are no physical orders or shipments. "Orders" in our system are plan purchases; each purchase generates an invoice. To view your purchases open Dashboard > Billing > Invoices. To add seats or change plan see "Upgrading and downgrading plans".
