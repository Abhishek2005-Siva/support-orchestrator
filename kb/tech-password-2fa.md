# Password reset and two-factor authentication

Use "Forgot password" on the login page; the reset link is valid for 30 minutes and works once. If you lost your authenticator device, use one of your 10 backup codes; if you have none, the workspace owner can reset your 2FA, or support can do it after identity verification (this takes up to 2 business days). Support never asks for your password.
