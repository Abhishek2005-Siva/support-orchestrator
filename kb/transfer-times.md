# How long transfers take

- **Internal transfers** between your own accounts are immediate.
- **ACH transfers** arrive within **1-3 business days**. Weekends and bank holidays do not count.
- **Wires** sent before the **17:00 ET** cut-off arrive the **same business day**.
- A transfer shows as **pending** until it is submitted to the payment network, then **submitted**, then **completed**.
- If an ACH transfer has not arrived after 3 business days, a specialist traces it.
