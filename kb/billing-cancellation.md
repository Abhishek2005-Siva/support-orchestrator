# Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel at any time from Dashboard > Billing > Cancel subscription. There is **no cancellation fee**. Access continues until the end of the paid period and no further charges are made. Your data is kept for 30 days after the end of the period, then permanently deleted. Cancelling does not automatically trigger a refund; refunds follow the refund policy.
