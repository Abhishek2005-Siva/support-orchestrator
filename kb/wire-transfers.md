# Wire transfers

- Wires sent before **17:00 ET** on a business day arrive the same day. Later wires go the next business day.
- Wires are **irrevocable once released**: they cannot be cancelled or recalled by us. Check the beneficiary details carefully.
- The wire fee is **$25** per wire. Wires of **$10,000 or more** may be reviewed by compliance before they are released.
- The daily wire limit is **$25,000** for standard customers.
