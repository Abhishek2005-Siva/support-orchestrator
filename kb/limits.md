# Daily limits

- Debit card spend limit: **$2,500** per day.
- ATM cash withdrawal limit: **$800** per day.
- Wire limit: **$25,000** per day for standard customers.
- A payment that would go over a limit is declined with the reason 'daily limit'. The limit resets at midnight.
- Private-segment customers can ask for higher limits.
