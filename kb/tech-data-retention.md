# Data retention and deletion

Event data is retained for 13 months on Pro and above and 3 months on Free and Starter. Deleted workspaces are purged after 30 days. Audit logs are kept 12 months. A data deletion request (GDPR/CCPA) is completed within 30 days and confirmed by email.
