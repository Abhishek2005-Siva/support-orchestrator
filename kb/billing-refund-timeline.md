# Refund timeline and status

After approval a refund is sent to your bank within 1 business day, and typically appears on your statement within 5-10 business days depending on the bank. Refund request statuses: **pending_approval** (waiting for a human specialist, used for amounts over $100), **approved** (sent to the bank) and **rejected** (not eligible). You receive an email when the status changes.
