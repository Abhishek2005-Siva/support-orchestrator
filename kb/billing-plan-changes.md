# Upgrading and downgrading plans

**Upgrade:** takes effect immediately; you are charged the prorated difference for the rest of the current cycle.
**Downgrade:** takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle; there is no partial refund for the unused part of the current cycle. Features above the new plan limits become read-only after the switch.
Switch between monthly and annual billing from Dashboard > Billing > Change plan. Moving to annual is charged immediately at 10x the monthly price.
