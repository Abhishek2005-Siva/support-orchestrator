# Returned or failed transfers

- A transfer is **returned** when the receiving bank rejects it. Common return codes: **R01** insufficient funds in the receiving account, **R02** account closed, **R03** no account found, **R04** invalid account number, **R10** receiver says the debit was not authorised.
- When a transfer is returned the money is credited back to your account; this appears as a 'Returned transfer' credit.
- Check the beneficiary name and account number and send again.
- A **failed** transfer never left your account.
