# Plans and pricing

Orbit offers five plans. Prices are in USD per month; annual billing costs 10 times the monthly price (two months free).

| Plan | Monthly price | Annual price | API requests/min | Seats |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 60 | 1 |
| Starter | $19 | $190 | 300 | 3 |
| Pro | $49 | $490 | 600 | 10 |
| Business | $149 | $1,490 | 3,000 | 50 |
| Enterprise | $499 | $4,990 | 10,000 | Unlimited |

SSO/SAML is available on Business and Enterprise. Priority support tiers: Business customers are Premium tier, Enterprise customers are VIP tier.
