# Using your card abroad

- Debit cards are **not enabled for international payments** by default. Turn them on in the app before you travel.
- A **foreign transaction fee of 2%** applies to payments in another currency. The exchange rate is the network rate on the day it posts.
- Payments abroad that you did not expect may be fraud: see report fraud.
