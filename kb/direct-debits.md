# Direct debits and recurring payments

- A recurring payment (subscription or direct debit) repeats at the same merchant, usually with the same amount each month.
- If you do not recognise a recurring payment, check earlier months first: the merchant may bill under a trading name.
- To stop a recurring payment, cancel with the merchant first; we can also block future payments to that merchant on request.
