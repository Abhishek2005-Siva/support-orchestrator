# Cancel a transfer

- A transfer can be cancelled only while it is **pending** and **has not been submitted** to the payment network.
- A transfer that is already submitted or completed cannot be cancelled. For an ACH transfer sent in error, ask the recipient to return it.
- **Wires cannot be cancelled.**
- A cancelled transfer releases the held amount back to your account immediately.
