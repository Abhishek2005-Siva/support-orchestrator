How it works

  1. Input guard cleans the message, masks card numbers and secrets, blocks injection and SQL attacks (no AI, milliseconds).
  2. Dispatcher decides what you need (payments, cards, general), how urgent and upset you are, and whether you asked us to act; legal, scam, bereavement and "get me a human" always escalate.
  3. Specialist agents (payments, cards & fraud, general) read the ledger and cards with tools that can only see your data.
  4. Verification asks the knowledge graph which checks and policies apply, runs them against the ledger with the policy table's numbers, and records what it consulted. Money decisions are code, never the AI.
  5. Actions (dispute, block, reverse, cancel) only run if you asked and a matching verification allows it.
  6. Validator re-checks every amount, date and id against the evidence; "credit issued" is only allowed if a credit really was posted.
  7. Human desk handles escalations and approvals; the graph pauses until a person decides.

An AI bank-support desk that verifies before it acts

A customer writes about a charge, a card or a transfer. AI agents read their real transactions, check the bank's own policies, regulations and knowledge graph, and only then act: file a dispute, block a card, reverse a fee, cancel a transfer. A fact-checker verifies every reply, and anything risky goes to a human.
