How a message is handled

  1. Input guard cleans the text, masks card numbers and secrets, and blocks injection and SQL attacks (milliseconds, no AI).
  2. Dispatcher works out what you need (billing, technical, general) and how urgent or upset you are. Legal threats, breaches and requests for a human always escalate.
  3. Specialists answer in parallel using real tools: your invoices, a refund-policy engine, error logs, service status and a knowledge base. They only ever see your account.
  4. Validator re-checks every amount, date, id and claim against the evidence. Anything unsupported is revised or sent to a human.
  5. Human review pauses risky cases in a queue. When staff approve, edit or reject, your chat is updated.

Everything here is synthetic. Answers take several seconds because the free model endpoint is slow, and each step is traced.
